## #faq — FAQ (HOMEPAGE SECTION)
## Common Questions About Working with Avia Code

**How much does a corporate website cost?**
Indicative starting price is IDR 3,000,000. The final quote varies with the design, number of pages, languages, content management and integrations.

**How long does custom development take?**
A basic website may take 1–3 weeks, while more complex dashboards and POS systems may require several weeks or months depending on scope and client inputs. Confirmed timelines are provided in the proposal.

**Do I need to prepare a technical specification?**
No. We can begin by understanding your business process, users and goals, then organize requirements through discovery.

**Can the website match my brand?**
Yes. Customization can range from package-based design refinement to a bespoke user interface.

**Are domain, hosting and maintenance included?**
Not by default. Infrastructure, API and long-term maintenance costs are itemized unless expressly included in the offer.

**Will I own the source code?**
Ownership and source-code delivery for custom projects are defined in the contract. SaaS access is typically licensed and does not automatically transfer source ownership.

**Do you offer revisions and bug fixes?**
Revision and warranty terms are agreed in writing. Our preliminary policy proposal includes two minor design revision rounds and a 14-day in-scope bug-fix period after handover.

**Can the system integrate with other software?**
Potentially, when the external service supports authorized integration and provides the necessary APIs and permissions.

**Is AI Office available?**
Avia AI Office is at the MVP stage. Demo/pilot availability depends on which capabilities have been validated.

**Can you test the security of any website?**
No. Security testing requires documented authorization, a clear scope and written engagement rules.
